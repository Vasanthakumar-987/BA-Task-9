1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing business sales data using Tableau. The dashboard provides a clear view of sales performance across different months, categories, products, and stores.

The main purpose of this project is to identify sales trends and understand how different business categories, products, and stores contribute to overall sales.

2. Objective
Analyze the overall sales performance of the business.
Identify sales trends over time.
Compare sales across different product categories.
Analyze the sales contribution of top products.
Compare sales performance between different stores.
Provide an interactive dashboard for easy data analysis.
3. Dataset

The dataset used for this project is Business Sales Trend Dataset.

Number of Records: 4,525

Dataset Columns
Column	Description
Date	Date of the sales transaction
Store	Name of the store
Category	Product category
Product	Product name
Quantity	Number of units sold
Unit Price	Price of a single unit
Discount (%)	Discount percentage applied
Sales	Total sales amount
4. Tools Used
Tableau Public – Data visualization and dashboard creation
CSV Dataset – Source of the business sales data
5. Dashboard Components

The dashboard contains the following visualizations:

Sales Trend Over Time

A line chart showing how total sales changed from month to month.

Sales by Category

A bar chart comparing total sales across different categories such as Electronics, Home Appliances, Fashion, Grocery, and Beauty.

Top 5 Products by Sales

A pie chart representing the sales contribution of the top products.

Sales by Store

A bar chart comparing total sales across different stores.

Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

Product
Category
Month of Date
Sales
Store

These filters allow users to interact with the dashboard and analyze specific portions of the dataset.

6. Key Insights

From the dashboard:

Electronics has the highest sales among the categories.
Sales fluctuate across the different months, with May showing the highest monthly sales in the displayed period.
Product sales are distributed across multiple products, allowing comparison of their contribution to overall sales.
Store-wise sales are relatively close to each other, with Trichy showing the highest sales among the displayed stores.
7. Dashboard Purpose

This dashboard can help businesses understand their sales performance and identify patterns across time, products, categories, and stores. The interactive filters also make it easier to perform detailed analysis based on specific requirements.

8. Conclusion

The Business Sales Analysis Dashboard provides a simple and interactive way to understand business sales data. Tableau was used to transform the raw dataset into meaningful visualizations that make sales trends and comparisons easier to understand.
